Critical Cardiac Safety & Medical Notice

Ibogaine and Noribogaine cause concentration-dependent QTc interval prolongation via hERG potassium channel blockade. Administration MUST ONLY occur under strict clinical supervision with continuous ECG monitoring, baseline CYP2D6 genotyping, and electrolyte optimization (potassium & IV magnesium).
